Tax freeFrom 1 Nov 2026 Japan uses a refund system, not tax off at the till. You pay the full price (¥5,000+ tax-excluded per shop per day still qualifies), keep the goods with you, then get the consumption tax back after a customs check at the airport you leave Japan from. For you that is Haneda (Delhi flight), not KIX — do it in the international departure lobby before baggage check-in. Bring passport and every item on each receipt. Ask the first big shop how their refund works, and leave extra time at Haneda.

The break-even rule

Leaving Japan, at Haneda

Pay the tax, claim it backFrom 1 Nov 2026 shops charge the full price with the 10% consumption tax. You get it back after a customs check at the airport you leave Japan from — for you that's Haneda, before NH837, not KIX. The National Tax Agency's Q&A (Q32, note 4) says domestic-to-international connections do it at the departure airport. Do it before bag drop, within 90 days of buying.
Everything on youThe check needs every tax-free item in hand, with the receipts. Your bags are checked through to Delhi at KIX, so watches, electronics and their receipts ride in your cabin bag. Leave extra time at Haneda.
Cosmetics, liquids and knives — the refund plan Two rules decide this (National Tax Agency Q&A). Q37: customs checks each receipt as a whole — if one item from it isn't with you at the Haneda kiosk, nothing on that receipt is refunded. Q39: open or use any cosmetic and it no longer counts.
  1. Keep everything sealed. Use what you brought from India until you're home.
  2. Small things go in your 1-litre liquids bag — each container 100 ml or less: Melano CC, Pair, the eye cream, the makeup mist. Spot patches are dry, so no rules at all. They stay with you the whole way, you show them at the Haneda kiosk, and they go through security with you.
  3. Bigger tubes and bottles (fino and TSUBAKI hair masks and refills, Naturie gel, Hada Labo, sheet-mask packs — they're soaked in serum): Japanese domestic flights have no 100 ml rule — cosmetics up to 0.5 L a container, 2 L each. Carry them in a separate bag on the KIX → Haneda flight, go out to the international departure lobby, do the customs check, then check that bag in at the ANA counter before security. You have about 2 h 40 at Haneda.
  4. Pay for the big ones separately. Ask the cashier for two receipts — small things on one, big tubes on the other — so if the big-tube plan falls through, only that receipt is lost.
  5. Or skip the refund on them. 10% of ¥5,000 of hair masks is about ₹270. Buy those without your passport, pack them in the KIX checked bag, and keep the refund for the things that ride with you.
  6. Knives must be checked, so they can't be shown at Haneda: buy them as a separate receipt, or without tax-free.
Ask ANA before you fly whether you can add a bag at Haneda when the rest is checked through from KIX — it's on your To-do.
Visit Japan WebAt Haneda you can do the customs check on your phone over the departure-lobby Wi-Fi instead of a kiosk, if you've registered on Visit Japan Web beforehand. Do it at home.
Small buys and payingUnder ¥5,000 before tax at one shop on one day gets no refund. Always pay in yen — if the terminal offers rupees, say no.

Arriving in Delhi

Allowance
₹75,000 per person, Baggage Rules 2026 (in force 2 Feb 2026). It can't be pooled — Arnav can't use Manan's.
Laptop
One new laptop or notepad each (18+), duty-free (Rule 5 proviso). Read as on top of the ₹75k — the 2016 rules said "over and above" in words, the 2026 text doesn't, so keep the receipt handy. A second laptop, or an iPad as well, counts against the ₹75k.
Alcohol, tobacco
Up to litres of alcohol and cigarettes. Beyond that it's dutied at much higher rates, outside this calculator.
Duty
% on the value above your allowance — not on everything. No surcharge on baggage since 2 Feb 2025.
Over the limit
Red Channel. Declare, show receipts, pay on the spot.
Opened boxes
Opening or using something doesn't make it duty-free. Customs looks at whether it's a new purchase.
Not declaring
Goods can be seized and released only on duty plus a redemption fine plus a penalty; large amounts can mean prosecution. Declaring is almost always cheaper.
Cash
Declare foreign currency notes over US$5,000, or over US$10,000 in all forms. Leaving Japan, declare cash over ¥1,000,000.

Your documents

Photograph your passport, visa and insurance and keep them here. They stay in this browser on this phone — they are never uploaded, never sent to the others, and they open with no signal.

Nothing leaves the phone. The image is shrunk and saved in this browser's own storage. There is no server in this app and nothing to upload to. The other two see nothing.

It is not a backup. Clearing your browser data, or deleting the site from your home screen, deletes these too. Keep the originals in your photos or a password manager as well.

Don't put them in the project folder. Anything sitting next to index.html gets published at a public, guessable web address when this deploys. That is the one place a passport scan must never be.

28–30 Nov

27 Nov
Odakyu Enoshima-Kamakura Freepass from Shinjuku, about ¥1,640. Covers the trip there and back and every Enoden ride.
Kansai
No rail pass. Kyoto → Osaka is around ¥580 on ICOCA, and nothing else in Kansai is long enough to make one pay.
3 Dec
Osaka Amazing Pass, 1 day. Umeda Sky is only free before 15:00, so go there first.
Cash
Keep ¥10,000 on you. Small restaurants, shrine entries and temple gates are often cash only. 7-Eleven ATMs take Indian cards.
