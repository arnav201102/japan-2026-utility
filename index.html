Your documents

Photograph your passport, visa and insurance and keep them here. They stay in this browser on this phone — they are never uploaded, never sent to the others, and they open with no signal.

Nothing leaves the phone. The image is shrunk and saved in this browser's own storage. There is no server in this app and nothing to upload to. The other two see nothing.

It is not a backup. Clearing your browser data, or deleting the site from your home screen, deletes these too. Keep the originals in your photos or a password manager as well.

Don't put them in the project folder. Anything sitting next to index.html gets published at a public, guessable web address when this deploys. That is the one place a passport scan must never be.

Passes and how to pay

Everywhere
Suica or ICOCA on your phone. Trains, buses, konbini, most vending machines.
25–29 Nov
Tokyo Subway 72-hour ticket. Metro and Toei only — the Kamakura day is JR, pay separately.
1–5 Dec
Kansai WIDE Area Pass. Carry your passport whenever you use it. Six free seat reservations — spend two on the Hashidate run.
5 Dec
Osaka Amazing Pass, 1 day. Umeda Sky is only free before 15:00, so go there first.
Cash
Keep ¥10,000 on you. Small restaurants, shrine entries and the Ine sea taxi are often cash only. 7-Eleven ATMs take Indian cards.
