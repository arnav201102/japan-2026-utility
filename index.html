Passes and how to pay

Everywhere
Suica or ICOCA on your phone. Trains, buses, konbini, most vending machines.
25–29 Nov
Tokyo Subway 72-hour ticket. Metro and Toei only — the Kamakura day is JR, pay separately.
1–5 Dec
Kansai WIDE Area Pass. Carry your passport whenever you use it. Six free seat reservations — spend two on the Hashidate run.
5 Dec
Osaka Amazing Pass, 1 day. Umeda Sky is only free before 15:00, so go there first.
Cash
Keep ¥10,000 on you. Small restaurants, shrine entries and the Ine sea taxi are often cash only. 7-Eleven ATMs take Indian cards.

Worth saying out loud

すみません — Sumimasen
Excuse me, and also sorry, and also thank you. Does most of the work.
これをください — Kore o kudasai
This one, please. Point at it.
英語のメニューはありますか — Eigo no menyū wa arimasu ka
Is there an English menu?
肉は入っていますか — Niku wa haitte imasu ka
Does this contain meat? Note that dashi (fish stock) is in almost everything.
お会計お願いします — Okaikei onegaishimasu
The bill, please. Usually you pay at the front counter, not the table.
駅はどこですか — Eki wa doko desu ka
Where is the station?
